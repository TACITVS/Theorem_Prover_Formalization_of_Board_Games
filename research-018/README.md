# Research 018: verified pairing strategies and adversarial tree certificates

**The empty-board game value is still unproved.** This work turns pairing
positions into rigorously proved nonlosing game-tree leaves and verifies a
local strategy tree covering every White move after a particular 15-round
history. It does not prove that Black can force that history or any safe leaf.

## Full continuation guarantee at pairing leaves

`QUBIC-PROOF-GATE-001/agent/SafePairing.v` verifies a partner function on the
125 cells. Each partner is in range and pairing is reciprocal; a cell paired
with itself is unpaired. The checker requires:

1. Every winning line already contains a Black stone or a nontrivial pair.
2. Whenever White owns a paired cell, Black owns its partner.

The proof establishes both facts needed for actual play: White cannot win on
the move before a response, and the concrete response policy restores the
invariant. A move on an unpaired cell or an already blocked pair allows any
legal reply. Otherwise Black takes its free partner. Illegal policy requests
are treated exactly as in the frozen rules, including fallback selection.

The resulting theorem quantifies over every remaining White strategy:

```coq
Theorem static_pairing_policy_nonloss : forall n st mate w,
  n + length (history st) = 125 -> valid_state st ->
  well_mated mate -> pairing_safe mate st ->
  outcome (step_game n true st w (static_pairing_policy mate)) <> WhiteWins.
```

This is stronger than assuming a board predicate stays true during future
play: it proves preservation and verifies an executable defensive policy.
The safe 10-round witness from the earlier work now has this complete
continuation theorem for all 105 remaining plies.

## Universal strategy-tree checker

`QUBIC-PROOF-GATE-001/agent/TreeCertificates.v` supplies two certificate forms:

- A pairing leaf, verified by the pairing checker and its policy theorem.
- A branching node supplying one Black reply and child certificate for each
  actual White move. The checker universally covers all 126 canonical policy
  requests (125 cells and an out-of-range representative), normalizing illegal
  requests through the frozen rules. Both players' immediate wins and a
  full-board draw are processed before considering a child.

The checked tree yields one total Black strategy against every White strategy.
The independent theorem `checked_empty_tree_proves_exact_draw` connects an
accepted certificate **at the empty board** to the exact locked draw goal,
using Research 017's first-player nonloss proof.

No such empty-board certificate is supplied. This is a conditional theorem
with an explicit, unproved certificate-checking premise.

## Checked local witness

`Witnesses.v` is generated by the untrusted `generate_witnesses.py`. It contains:

- A legal, nonterminal 10-round history and its pairing table, with a proved
  concrete defensive policy against every continuation.
- A legal, nonterminal 15-round history from an earlier witness schedule.
  There are 95 empty cells. For every White choice the certificate supplies a
  Black reply and a checked pairing table. The resulting nonloss guarantee
  covers all 95 remaining plies, not merely the next round.
- Explicit negative checks: the safe10 table and the local strategy tree both
  fail when applied to the empty board.

All branches and leaf data are kernel-checked. The generator uses a deterministic
bipartite matching search over independently generated geometric lines; its
implementation and successful exit status are not trusted proof evidence.

The histories are **existential reachable examples**, not histories reachable
against every adversarial White policy. Establishing that global quantifier is
the decisive unfinished task. Passing this research suite does not pass the
global specification-locked proof gate.

## Reproduce

```powershell
python research-018/verify.py
```

This reproduces the witness source, checks the unchanged trusted-source hashes,
compiles from source in a fresh temporary directory, checks the assumptions of
14 selected results, and runs ordinary recursive `rocq check`. It never weakens
or edits the trusted global acceptance gate. To regenerate the witness source:

```powershell
python research-018/generate_witnesses.py
```
